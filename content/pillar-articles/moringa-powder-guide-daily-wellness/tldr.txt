Moringa powder is dried, ground moringa leaf — a daily-use superfood for vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants when used consistently in small amounts.
Add ½–1 teaspoon to smoothies, chutneys, or warm water with lemon; heat-sensitive — stir in after cooking when possible.
Pick lab-tested, leaf-only moringa (not stem filler) such as Sarvital moringa powder for purity you can verify.

Q: What is moringa powder good for?
A: Moringa leaf powder is used as a daily superfood for broad micronutrients and antioxidants. People add it for general wellness, energy support, and greens in a vegetarian diet — not as a treatment for disease.

Q: How do I use moringa powder daily?
A: Start with ½ teaspoon in a smoothie or yogurt, then move to 1 teaspoon per day if you tolerate it well. You can also mix into chutneys, sprinkle on salads, or stir into warm (not boiling) lemon water.

Q: Can I cook moringa powder at high heat?
A: High heat can reduce some heat-sensitive nutrients. Add moringa at the end of cooking or use it in room-temperature foods like smoothies and chutneys for best results.

Q: Is moringa safe during pregnancy?
A: Moringa is widely consumed as food in many regions, but pregnancy needs individualized advice. Talk to your doctor or dietitian before starting any new supplement routine while pregnant or breastfeeding.

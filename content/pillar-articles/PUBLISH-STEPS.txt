P2-B — Publish two pillar articles (Shopify Admin)

Prerequisites
- Blog created: handle "wellness", title "Wellness" (Online Store > Blog posts > Manage blogs)
- Metafield definitions on Articles (Settings > Custom data > Articles):
  - custom.article_tldr (Multi-line text)
  - custom.article_faq (Multi-line text)
  - custom.article_product (Product reference)
- Theme pushed with article.pillar template

For each article in content/pillar-articles/manifest.json (publish Sattu FIRST):

1. Online Store > Blog posts > Add blog post (Wellness blog)
2. Title: see manifest "title"
3. URL handle: see manifest "handle"
4. Theme template: pillar (article.pillar)
5. Search engine listing:
   - SEO title: see article folder or manifest
   - Meta description: see manifest
6. Content: paste body.html from article folder into rich text editor (HTML mode)
7. Metafields on the article:
   - article_tldr: paste tldr.txt (3 lines)
   - article_faq: paste faq.txt
   - article_product: select product from manifest "product_handle"
8. Add featured image (product or lifestyle)
9. Publish

Verify after both are live:
  node scripts/verify-pillar-articles.mjs

Gate P2-B: 2 URLs indexed; combined >=20 organic clicks/month from non-brand queries within 90 days of publish.

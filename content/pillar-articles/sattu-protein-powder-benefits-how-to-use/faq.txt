Q: Is sattu a complete protein powder?
A: Sattu provides meaningful plant protein along with iron, fiber, and slow-release carbs from roasted gram. It is not identical to whey isolate, but it is a whole-food Indian staple many families use for daily nutrition — especially vegetarian and fitness-focused diets.

Q: How much sattu should I take per day?
A: Most people start with 2–3 tablespoons (about 20–30 g) once or twice a day in sharbat, chillas, or smoothies. Adjust based on activity level and how your body responds. If you have a medical condition, ask your clinician.

Q: Can sattu replace whey protein after workouts?
A: Sattu can support post-workout recovery as part of a balanced meal with carbs and hydration, especially if you prefer plant-based or traditional foods. Athletes with very high protein targets may still combine sattu with other protein sources.

Q: What is the best way to make sattu sharbat?
A: Mix 2 tbsp sattu with water, a pinch of salt, lemon juice, and roasted cumin. Sweeten lightly if you like. Drink chilled — it is a classic summer drink in North India and an easy way to add protein without cooking.

Q: How do I know sattu powder is good quality?
